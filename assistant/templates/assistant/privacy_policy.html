Privacy Policy

Bilal AI Assistant

Bilal AI Assistant helps generate automated replies to WhatsApp messages. This page explains how basic message information may be handled when someone contacts the assistant.

Information processed

WhatsApp messages and phone numbers may be processed only to understand the incoming request and generate an automated reply. Processing is limited to operating and improving the assistant conversation experience.

No sale of data

Bilal AI Assistant does not sell WhatsApp messages, phone numbers, or other user data.

Data deletion requests

Users may request deletion of their stored data at any time. To request deletion, email bilalshafqat35@gmail.com with the WhatsApp phone number related to the request.
